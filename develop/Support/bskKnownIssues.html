Basilisk Known Issues

This page records issues affecting tagged Basilisk releases, including their fixes and workarounds. Issues introduced and fixed entirely within a beta cycle need no entry if they never affected a tagged release. Pending entries describe fixes for issues in tagged releases or limitations that will ship in the next tagged release.

Select a version series to read its known issues. Each page lists individual releases newest first, including the development version where applicable.
