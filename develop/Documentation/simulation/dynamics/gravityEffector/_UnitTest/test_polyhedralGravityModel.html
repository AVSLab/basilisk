test_polyhedralGravityModel

Validate polyhedral gravity normalization, initialization, and energy.

A closed C-shaped body contains fourteen equal cubes. Its signed volume is 14 km^3, whereas summing the absolute tetrahedral volumes gives 18 km^3. Acceleration and potential are checked against the superposition of separate cube models and their point-mass limits. Stored Eros results check compatibility with a mesh whose tetrahedral contributions are all positive (issue 1681). Potential gradients, inertial-frame evaluations, and spacecraft energy checks cover the sign and reference-frame corrections in issue 1689.

test_polyhedralGravityModel.test_initialization_retry_matches_fresh_model(invalid_volume, through_body, previously_initialized)[source]

Recover from a rejected mesh without retaining facet or edge dyads.

After rejection, replace the mesh with a valid cube and compare acceleration and potential against a fresh model. An optional earlier successful initialization also populates the edge cache, which a rejected mesh does not rebuild. Cyclically permuting the earlier vertices changes the cached dyads associated with each index while preserving a valid, outward-oriented cube.

Parameters:

  • invalid_volume – Rejected mesh configuration.

  • through_body – Whether all initialization attempts use GravBodyData.

  • previously_initialized – Whether to initialize a valid mesh before rejection.

Check energy at the output epoch for moving, rotating gravity bodies.
