test_lambertSolverMultiRev.test_gooding_multirevolution_branches(lam, revolutions, time_ratio)[source]

Check both roots against independent bracketing over near and distant transfers.

The geometry parameter covers short and long transfers, including the issue 1651 geometry. Time ratios cover the neighborhood of the minimum, both starter patches, and long transfers. The lam = -0.99 case exercises a minimum-time Halley step that leaves the elliptic domain without a safeguard. Each root must be on its own side of the minimum and satisfy the independently evaluated flight-time equation.

test_lambertSolverMultiRev.test_minimum_time_boundary(method, direction, lam, revolutions)[source]

Check infeasible, coalesced, and distinct roots on successive module updates.

At the minimum, both valid slots represent the same orbit. Values below the minimum are invalid, and values above it must recover two distinct roots. Reusing the module also checks recovery after an infeasible request.

test_lambertSolverMultiRev.test_rotated_polar_direction_roundoff(method, direction, revolutions, azimuth)[source]

Preserve the polar tie under rotation with unequal endpoint radii.

The unrotated endpoints have an exactly zero cross-product z-component, but normalizing them separately introduces a small negative component. Both solution branches must follow the documented tie convention and agree with an axis-aligned reference rotated about the input-frame z-axis.
