Related Publications

These selected papers describe Basilisk’s architecture, modeling capabilities, and applications, including Vizard visualization and BSK-RL spacecraft autonomy. The selection includes research using these tools and dynamics formulations that underpin Basilisk models, such as the backsubstitution method. Papers are grouped by publication year, with the newest years first in each section. Conference and journal versions are listed separately when both are included.

This is not an exhaustive bibliography of research using Basilisk. Additional publications are available in Hanspeter Schaub’s journal paper list and conference paper list.
