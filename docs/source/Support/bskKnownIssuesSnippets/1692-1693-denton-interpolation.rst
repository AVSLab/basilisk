- :ref:`dentonFluxModel` in Basilisk 2.12.2 reads beyond its local-time table between 23 h and 24 h, producing incorrect electron and ion fluxes and a discontinuity at midnight (#1692). At whole-hour local times, it also selects the lower energy bin instead of interpolating in energy (#1693). The fixes wrap local time and table lookups at midnight and restore whole-hour energy interpolation. Recompute affected flux histories with the corrected module; the model data files and configuration are unchanged.
