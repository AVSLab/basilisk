- In Basilisk 2.12.2, :ref:`dentonFluxModel` accepts 41 through 50 output energies despite having space for only 40 in its internal energy array. These counts cause out-of-bounds accesses and incorrect output energies and fluxes. A count of zero also reaches an integer division by zero. The internal output-energy array now matches the flux message capacity, and initialization rejects counts outside 1 through 50. On affected versions, use 1 through 40 output energies.
